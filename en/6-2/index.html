Code of Conduct and whistleblowing channel

The Benfica SAD Code of Conduct (in force since 23 November 2022) states that the physical and psychological well-being of the people who work at Benfica is of fundamental importance. It prohibits harassment and discrimination at work and protects those who report it.

  • Among other things, degrading or humiliating comments, intimidating or threatening behaviour, and ignoring, isolating or excluding a colleague are unacceptable
  • Anyone who knows of or has well-founded suspicions of harassment must report it
  • Those who report it, and the witnesses they name, cannot be disciplined for doing so, unless they acted in bad faith

Internal, confidential whistleblowing channel: slbenficasad.integrityline.com (opens in a new window)

Note

(Information not available in this demo version) This is the Benfica SAD Code. Confirm whether the same code and the same channel apply to all employees of the Club and of the other entities of the Benfica Group.

Occupational Medicine

All workers are entitled to Occupational Medicine, in addition to the mandatory periodic check-ups:

  • Occasional Examination at the Employee’s Request
  • Occasional Examination at the Physician’s Initiative
  • Occasional Examination at the Company’s Request

These appointments can be requested through Human Resources or the Occupational Health and Safety service. (Information not available in this demo version) At very busy times, such as weeks with several matches, it may not be possible to get an appointment on the same day.

It is important to know

If Occupational Medicine cannot respond within the expected time, an appropriate alternative should be activated (e.g. the internal employee support line (Information not available in this demo version), SNS 24 – 808 24 24 24, option 4 –, psychological support or an external resource), so that the response to the employee is not delayed.

A final word

Looking after mental health at work does not require perfect answers or clinical training.

Above all, it takes the willingness to observe, listen and connect people with the right support. Small gestures – an attentive question, a safe space to talk, the courage not to ignore the distress of the person next to us, at the Stadium, at the Campus or in the office – make all the difference.

PFA is human, close and practical support for someone who is struggling – in a way that always respects their dignity, their culture and their abilities. It does not replace clinical care, but it is often the first step that makes the difference.

Keep this material as a resource you can come back to whenever you need it. And remember that looking after others starts with looking after yourself too.

Thank you for helping to build a more attentive, safer and healthier workplace for everyone who makes Benfica. E pluribus unum: out of many, one.
